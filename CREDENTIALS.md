## How to Test on the Web Portal

1. Navigate to the **Sign In** page at:
   ```
   http://localhost:5173/login
   ```
2. In the **"Preset Test Credentials"** panel on the left side, click on any role card (e.g., *Startup / Innovator* or *Gov Department*).
3. The role, email, and password will be automatically filled into the login form.
4. Click **"Log In To Secure System"** to directly access that role's dashboard.
